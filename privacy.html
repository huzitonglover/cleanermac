CleanerMac 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日 · 适用版本：CleanerMac 1.0 及以后版本（含菜单栏助手 CleanerMacMenu）

简要说明

  • 我们不收集任何数据。不需要注册或登录，没有账号体系。
  • 扫描和清理全部在你的 Mac 上完成，文件名、路径、文件内容都不会离开你的电脑。
  • 没有统计分析、广告或任何第三方 SDK，也不追踪你。
  • 唯一的联网操作是你主动点「测速」时，由 macOS 自带工具连接 Apple 的测速服务器。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

无。CleanerMac 不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人信息或使用数据，包括但不限于：姓名、邮箱、设备标识符、IP 地址、位置、文件列表、浏览记录、崩溃日志或使用统计。

App Store 的「App 隐私」标签中，CleanerMac 标注为「未收集数据」。

2. 应用在本机读取哪些信息，用来做什么

为了实现清理和监控功能，应用会在你的 Mac 上本地读取以下信息。它们只在内存中处理或保存在本机，不会发送给我们或任何第三方：

  • 文件和文件夹信息（名称、大小、修改/创建时间）：用于找出系统垃圾、缓存、日志、大文件、重复文件、旧安装包和应用残留，并在列表中显示。
  • 已安装的应用和启动项：用于卸载残留清理和启动项管理。
  • 系统状态（CPU、内存、存储空间、电池、网络流量、开机时长）：用于在主程序和菜单栏助手中显示，以及在开机超过一周时建议重启。
  • 应用设置：你的偏好设置和磁盘授权书签，保存在本机的 UserDefaults 和 App Group 中，供主程序和菜单栏助手共享。

3. 权限说明

  • 沙盒：CleanerMac 运行在 Apple App 沙盒中，只能访问你明确授权的位置。
  • 磁盘访问：需要扫描应用容器以外的内容时，应用会通过系统标准的打开面板请你授权（通常是启动磁盘）。授权以安全书签的形式保存在本机，你可以随时在应用「设置」中撤销。
  • 完全磁盘访问（可选）：不授予也能正常使用，只是少数受系统保护的文件夹（如邮件、Safari 数据）无法扫描。你可以在「系统设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 完全磁盘访问」中随时开启或关闭。
  • 登录项：菜单栏助手注册为登录项，可在应用设置或「系统设置 › 通用 › 登录项」中关闭。

4. 文件删除

清理前，应用都会先列出要处理的项目并请你确认。默认情况下文件会移到废纸篓，你可以从废纸篓恢复。只有以下情况会永久删除，并且同样会先经你确认：清空废纸篓、应用会自动重建的系统缓存和日志、Xcode 编译缓存和设备日志。应用不会删除系统文件。

5. 网络连接

CleanerMac 主程序不联网。菜单栏助手只有在你点击「测速」时，才会运行 macOS 自带的 /usr/bin/networkQuality，连接 Apple 的测速服务器以测量网速。这一过程由 Apple 提供，适用 Apple 隐私政策；测速结果只显示在本机，我们不会收到。

6. 第三方服务

CleanerMac 不包含任何第三方 SDK、统计分析、广告或崩溃上报服务。应用通过 Mac App Store 分发，购买和下载由 Apple 处理，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。如果你在系统中开启了「与 App 开发者共享」诊断数据，Apple 可能会向开发者提供匿名的崩溃报告，这由 macOS 控制，你可以在「系统设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 分析与改进」中关闭。

7. 儿童隐私

CleanerMac 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁（或你所在地区规定年龄）以下的儿童。

8. 你的权利

由于我们不收集也不持有你的任何数据，没有需要查询、更正、导出或删除的个人数据。删除应用即可清除它在本机保存的设置；你也可以随时撤销磁盘授权和登录项。

如果你通过邮件联系我们，我们只会用你提供的邮箱和内容来回复你的问题，不会用于其他用途，也不会提供给第三方；问题处理完后你可以要求我们删除往来邮件。

9. 政策更新

如本政策有变更，我们会更新本页面并修改上方的生效日期。如果变更涉及开始收集数据，我们会在应用内另行告知并征得你的同意。

10. 联系我们

对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请发邮件至 345499860@qq.com，我们通常会在 3 个工作日内回复。

CleanerMac Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · Applies to CleanerMac 1.0 and later, including the CleanerMacMenu menu bar helper

In short

  • We collect no data. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Scanning and cleaning happen entirely on your Mac. File names, paths and contents never leave your computer.
  • No analytics, ads or third-party SDKs, and no tracking.
  • The only network activity is the optional speed test, which uses a built-in macOS tool to reach Apple's test servers when you click "Test Speed".

1. Information we collect

None. CleanerMac does not collect, store or transmit any personal information or usage data, including names, email addresses, device identifiers, IP addresses, location, file lists, browsing history, crash logs or usage statistics.

On the App Store privacy label, CleanerMac is listed as "Data Not Collected".

2. What the app reads on your Mac, and why

To provide its features, the app reads the following locally. It is processed in memory or kept on your Mac, and is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • File and folder information (name, size, modification/creation date): to find system junk, caches, logs, large and duplicate files, old installers and app leftovers, and to show them in lists.
  • Installed apps and launch items: for leftover cleanup and launch item management.
  • System status (CPU, memory, storage, battery, network throughput, uptime): shown in the app and the menu bar helper, and used to suggest a restart after a week of uptime.
  • App settings: your preferences and disk access bookmark, stored locally in UserDefaults and an App Group shared with the menu bar helper.

3. Permissions

  • Sandbox: CleanerMac runs in the Apple App Sandbox and can only access locations you explicitly grant.
  • Disk access: to scan beyond its container, the app asks you to grant access (usually to the startup disk) through the standard macOS open panel. Access is kept as a security-scoped bookmark on your Mac and can be revoked in the app's Settings.
  • Full Disk Access (optional): the app works without it; a few protected folders (such as Mail and Safari data) just can't be scanned. You can turn it on or off in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access.
  • Login item: the menu bar helper is registered as a login item and can be turned off in the app's Settings or in System Settings › General › Login Items.

4. File removal

Before anything is removed, the app lists the items and asks you to confirm. By default files are moved to the Trash, so you can restore them. Files are permanently deleted only in these cases, also after your confirmation: emptying the Trash, system caches and logs that apps rebuild automatically, and Xcode build caches and device logs. The app never removes system files.

5. Network connections

The main CleanerMac app makes no network connections. The menu bar helper runs the built-in macOS tool /usr/bin/networkQuality only when you click "Test Speed"; it connects to Apple's test servers to measure your connection. This service is provided by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. Results are shown only on your Mac and are never sent to us.

6. Third-party services

CleanerMac contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising or crash reporting. It is distributed through the Mac App Store, where purchases and downloads are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. If you have enabled "Share with app developers" in macOS, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports to the developer; this is controlled by macOS in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

7. Children's privacy

CleanerMac does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the age required in your region).

8. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold any of your data, there is no personal data to access, correct, export or delete. Deleting the app removes the settings it stored on your Mac, and you can revoke disk access and the login item at any time.

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you, never for anything else, and never share them. You can ask us to delete the correspondence once your question is resolved.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change would involve collecting any data, we will tell you in the app and ask for your consent first.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email 345499860@qq.com. We usually reply within 3 business days.
